Our Legacy

The Craft of Living Art

Established near the historic jasmine fields of the Côte d’Azur, Maison Élixir honors nearly a century of unhurried craftsmanship. Unlike mass-manufactured fragrances, every drop of our perfume is formulated with reverence for time.

We practice ancient cold-enfleurage and supercritical carbon-dioxide extraction to capture the freshest, most authentic scent profiles possible from fragile botanical blooms.

Ethical Botanicals

Sustainably cultivated in partner cooperatives across Provence, Madagascar, and Calabria.

Zero Animal Testing

Cruelty-free certified, using plant-based and synthetic nature-identical musk substitutes.
